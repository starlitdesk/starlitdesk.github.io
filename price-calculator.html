How the numbers work

Labour = hours × hourly rate. Overhead = overhead % of materials + labour. Price = (cost + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − fee % − margin %). Fees and profit are then worked out from the rounded price, so the lines always add up to what is shown. Read the guide to pricing handmade items for a worked example.
