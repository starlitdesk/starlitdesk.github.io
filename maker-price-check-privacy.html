Maker Price Check: privacy

Privacy statement for the Maker Price Check browser extension, version 0.1.0. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Maker Price Check does not collect, send or sell any data.

  • The numbers you type stay in your browser. The extension saves your last inputs with Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) so they are there the next time you open it. You can remove them with "Clear all" or by removing the extension.
  • The extension makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no remote code.
  • It asks for one permission, storage, used only for the saving described above. It cannot read the pages you visit.
  • The footer has one link to a free guide on starlitdesk.github.io. It opens only if you click it.
