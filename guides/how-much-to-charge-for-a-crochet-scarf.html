How much to charge for a crochet scarf, by yarn weight

To work out how much to charge for a crochet scarf, cost the yarn in the skeins you buy, time the scarf including finishing, pay those hours, then price for fees and margin. Yarn weight decides most of it: in our illustrative example a 2-hour chunky scarf has a full-cost price of $70, while an 8.75-hour DK scarf needs $215, and the same $35 pays $6.11 an hour on the chunky scarf but $0.37 on the DK one.

1. Why yarn weight sets the price

A scarf is long, flat and simple, so the stitches are not what makes one cost more than another. The yarn weight is. One crochet designer puts it plainly: "Chunky yarn and super bulky scarves will work up much faster than scarves worked in fingering weight yarn or dk yarn." (ChristaCoDesign, updated 21 Sep 2026). Thicker yarn usually costs more per scarf, but it saves hours, and hours are the larger cost at any fair hourly rate. That is why the general advice in how to price crochet and knitted items, that chunkier yarn makes the same skill earn more per hour, matters so much for scarves.

2. Time one scarf, including finishing and packing

Published times are a starting point, not your number. The same designer writes: "It might take some crocheters about 2 hours to crochet a narrow 4-5″ scarf that’s 60″ long, like this one." and "It’s likely most crocheters could make a simple scarf in chunky yarn in about 1 -2 hours." A super bulky pattern on Crochet Dreamz (updated 8 Jun 2026) uses "Approximately 220 yards. I used 2 skeins and then a little from the third skein. Each skein was 90 yards long." and its maker says: "I crocheted this gorgeous chunky scarf in just under an hour and a half and I am truly excited about how it turned out."

Crocheting is not the whole job. A pricing article on a website-builder's blog (Hostinger, 10 Sep 2026) counts the rest: "If a scarf takes 3 hours to crochet, 30 minutes to finish, and 15 minutes to package, your total labor time is 3.75 hours." It also warns that pricing as a multiple of materials "doesn't directly account for how long an item takes to make." Time your own next scarf from first chain to packed, with ends woven in and fringe or tassels attached.

Fees = price × 9.5%, rounded to the cent, + $0.45. Profit = price − fees − full cost. Per hour worked = (profit + labour) ÷ hours.

For the chunky scarf: (49.00 + 0.45) ÷ 0.905 = $54.64 to break even, and (49.00 + 0.45) ÷ (1 − 0.095 − 0.15) = $65.50 for a 15% margin, so $70.00. The DK scarf takes more than four times as long, so the same method gives $215.00.

6. How many scarves before Christmas

Count your hours before you take orders. From Mon 19 Oct to Mon 14 Dec is 8 weeks. At 8 crochet hours a week that is 64.00 hours: 32 chunky scarves, or 19 scarf and hat sets, or 7 DK scarves. For delivery by Christmas in the lower 48 states, USPS lists a send-by date of "Thursday, Dec. 17" for Ground Advantage (USPS holiday shipping dates, read Tue 13 Oct 2026), so finishing on Mon 14 Dec leaves three days to pack and post. Common questions

How much should I sell a crochet scarf for? At least your floor from yarn, hours and fees. In our example that is $70 for a chunky scarf, $90 for worsted and $215 for DK at $15.00 an hour.

How much can I sell a crochet scarf for? Whatever your buyers pay; the question is what that price pays you. At $35 the example pays $6.11 an hour for chunky and $0.37 for DK.

How much to charge for a hand knit scarf? Use the same method. Knitting speed differs from crochet, so time your own scarf rather than using these hours.

How long does it take to crochet a scarf? One designer says about 1 to 2 hours for a simple chunky scarf and about 2 hours for a narrow one; add finishing and packing, then time your own.

Selling a scarf made from someone else's pattern? Read that pattern's terms for selling finished items first. Pricing a blanket instead? See how much to charge for a crochet blanket.

Pricing a sweater? See how much to charge for a crochet sweater, size by size, where the hours, not the yarn, set the price.
