Wedding vendor payment schedule: when each deposit and balance is due

A wedding vendor payment schedule lists every deposit and every balance with its due date, then adds them up by month. Deposits are usually paid when you sign; most balances fall due 14 to 30 days before the wedding, so the last month is the expensive one. In the illustrative example below, deposits are 27.52% of the $25,950.00 total and 69.39% falls due in the last 45 days.

What goes in a payment schedule

One row per vendor, with seven columns:

  • Vendor and the contract total.
  • Deposit and the date you paid it (usually the day you sign).
  • Balance (total minus deposit) and its due date, from the contract.
  • Paid date, typed in when you pay, so you can see at a glance what is still open.

Some vendors split the balance into more than one payment; give each payment its own line with its own date. Whether a payment is refundable depends on your contract; check it before you sign.

Typical deposit sizes and balance due dates

These are ranges published by wedding sites, not rules. Your contracts decide.

  • Deposits: a planner's guide gives venue 25%–50%, catering 25%–50%, photography and video 25%–50% or a flat retainer, florist 20%–30%, attire 50% or more, and rentals 25% (The Yes Girls, Jul 2026).
  • Balances: final payments are often due 30 days before the wedding for photography, videography and music, and 14 days before for catering, florals and lighting (The Knot, updated Aug 2024). The same planner's guide puts it plainly: "Nearly every remaining balance comes due in the last month, and often the last two weeks."
  • Couples' experience: on wedding forums, couples report caterer deposits from a flat $250 to 50% of the estimated total (WeddingWire forum) and venue deposits of 25%, 30% and 50% (WeddingWire.ca forum). These are individual couples' contracts.

Step 2: turn "14 days before" into real dates

Contracts often give the balance as "30 days before the event". Count back on a calendar and write the real date in your schedule, because "30 days before" is easy to miss when it falls on a weekday. Here, 12 June 2027 minus 30 days is Thursday 13 May; minus 14 days is Saturday 29 May.

Step 4: check your set-aside against the schedule

If you put money aside each month, compare the running total with what is due by each month end. This is arithmetic, not advice on how to save.

Splitting $25,950.00 evenly over the nine months from October to June gives about $2,883.33 a month (nine of those make $25,949.97, $0.03 short of the total). By the end of May that is 8 × $2,883.33 = $23,066.64, but $25,350.00 is due by 29 May: $2,283.36 short in the peak month.

Day-of payments and tips

Some hair and makeup artists and musicians are paid on the day, and tips are often handed over in envelopes on the day (The Knot). Give each its own line with the wedding date, so the cash is counted in the schedule rather than found missing on the morning.

Keeping it current

  • Type the paid date the day you pay; a blank paid date is an open payment.
  • When the final head count is in, update the catering total: the balance changes, the deposit you paid does not.
  • Re-add the month totals after every change, and check the next 60 days each month.

Common questions

When do you pay wedding vendors? On each contract's due dates: usually a deposit at signing and the balance 14 to 30 days before the wedding, as above. List every date in one schedule so none is missed.

What percentage deposit does a wedding venue take? The ranges published above run from 25% to 50%, and couples report 25%, 30% and 50%. Your venue's contract sets the figure.

How much deposit do caterers want? It may be a flat amount or a percentage of the estimated total; the balance is often tied to the final head count, so expect it to change.

Is a deposit refundable? That depends on your contract. Check it before you sign.

Make things for weddings yourself? The seller's side of the same arithmetic is in how much deposit to take on a custom order; photographers can see why their balance is due weeks ahead in how to price photography sessions.
